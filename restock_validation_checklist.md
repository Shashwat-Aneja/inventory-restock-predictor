Inventory forecast review checklist

- [ ] Confirm day values are consistent period indices and stock counts reflect the same unit.
- [ ] Check duplicate-day aggregation is appropriate for the source data.
- [ ] Compare forecasts with a chronological holdout and a simple baseline.
- [ ] Include expected receipts and supplier lead time in the replenishment decision outside this baseline model.
- [ ] Review false alarms and missed stockouts before changing the threshold.
- [ ] Re-evaluate error after demand patterns or inventory policies change.

A trend forecast is not a substitute for lead-time demand and safety-stock analysis.
